Pop! Eight ways to wipe a session

Flush is becoming Pop. Eight concepts for the button, each in three forms: a rich drawing (rendered in code with Remotion), a line icon for the nav bar (24-unit grid, 1.9 stroke, ink on the corn-yellow button), and a toolbar you can play with. Tap the yellow button in each phone (concept 2: press and hold). Every phone has a dark and a light theme.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the phones skip the animations and cut straight to the result.

My top 3

  1. Hold to Heat
    The only concept that fixes a real problem: a hold makes an accidental Pop nearly impossible with no dialog, and the button heating up under your thumb is the most satisfying thing on this page.
  2. Kernel Pop
    The most honest picture of the word "Pop", it reads at 24 px, and the wobble then burst is a cheap animation in Compose; pair it with Hold to Heat's hold and you have the button.
  3. Nibbled Cob
    The funniest idea here (the old cartoon typewriter-corn gag, "ding!" and all) and a genuinely new icon nobody else has; it's slower, so it suits the long-press or the menu's "Pop everything".

Honourable mention: Corny Shh is the most on-brand, since it is the logo, but a face at 24 px is a gamble.
