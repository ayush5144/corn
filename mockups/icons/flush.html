Flush icons

The app stays Corn; the feature stays Flush (founder, 2026-11-12). Eight fun line icons (six Corn-flavoured, two not) for the Flush Button, drawn to Corn's icon rules (24 grid, 1.9 stroke, round ends), in ink on the corn-yellow button, next to the Kernel Safe. Tap a yellow button to play its little animation.
