Pop & Vault icons

Concepts for the renamed Pop button and a Corn-style Vault, made locally with FLUX.2 klein 4B (mflux). Each one is shown large, then at real size in the bottom toolbar (Back · Vault · Tabs · Pop · Menu: 24 dp icons, 46×40 dp Pop button) and at 48/24 px. Chosen ones get redrawn as clean vectors.

Pop button, two ways: on dark, the colour art on a dark button with a yellow ring; on light, the art as a one-colour silhouette on today's yellow fill (how a vector glyph would ship). Colour art on the yellow fill doesn't work: yellow on yellow.

Pop: go with Burst. It says "pop" in one glance and keeps a bold shape at 24 px. Ship it as colour art on a dark button with a yellow ring (the one-colour silhouette on today's yellow fill loses the kernel), or redraw it two-tone: dark puff, kernel cut out.
Vault: go with Corn Crib. It's the one idea that's both clearly "storage" and clearly Corn, and its roof-and-slats glyph survives at 24 px and with + / ✓ badges. The vector redraw needs deep-corn slats instead of cream so it holds up on the light theme. Kernel Safe is the safe fallback if "locked" must read louder.
