Line icons

Founder (2026-11-12): nav-bar icons as lines only, like Back, Tabs and Menu. Same rules as Corn's icons today: 24-unit grid, 1.9 stroke, round ends, 18 units tall. These SVG paths are the ones that would go into the app (ui/Icons.kt). The Pop glyph sits in ink on the corn-yellow Pop button, like today's swirl.

From open-source icon sets

Drawn by professional icon designers on the same 24-unit grid. Free to use in the app with a licence credit (Lucide: ISC; Phosphor: MIT). Both draw popcorn as a bucket, which is the standard "popcorn" icon.
