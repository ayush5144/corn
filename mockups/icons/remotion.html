Pop & Vault, drawn in code

The AI concepts, redrawn by hand in React + SVG with Remotion and rendered to 1024 px PNGs: layered shapes, gradients, bevels (SVG filters) and one light from the top-left. Three variations each, then real size in the toolbar (Back · Vault · Tabs · Pop · Menu: 24 dp icons, 46×40 dp Pop button) on dark and light, and at 48/24 px.

Founder's direction: every Pop concept sits on the chunky push button from the Flush button exploration: cream base ring, dark well, domed top with a gloss, two signal arcs. The Pop art is the button's face. In the toolbar the whole button is the Pop button.

The pop, animated

Burst, variation c: press, spring back, the kernel jumps and the puff blooms. 1.2 s at 60 fps, a Remotion <Composition> rendered to MP4.

The Pop button

Variations: a corn dome, art printed two-tone (ink + cream, like the swirl on the blue button) · b night dome, art in full colour, leaf arcs · c corn dome, the art popping up off it, bigger.

The Vault

Each with its three uses, Vault, Save to Vault (+) and Saved (✓), all drawn in code: the badge cuts a clean gap out of the icon so it works on any background.
