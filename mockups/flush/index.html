Why “Flush”

The name is the feature. Corn needed a joke to explain the Flush Button. Flush is the Flush Button. One word says “wipe it, start fresh”.

The mark is the moment. The bright-blue whirlpool is the second after you press: everything spirals away. Water reads as clean, not dirty, so the toilet humour stays a wink.

Discreet on a home screen. An adult browser's icon gets seen by friends, family and coworkers. A blue swirl looks like a weather app or a game. Nobody asks.

Voice: cool, quick, cheeky. Toilet jokes about your tracks only. Never what's in the bowl.

Type

flush Fredoka 600

Fredoka is rounded and bubbly, like letters made of water, so the lowercase wordmark reads friendly and a little silly without being childish. UI text stays in Figtree, which the app already uses, so only the display face changes (it replaces Bricolage).
