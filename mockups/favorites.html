Favourites: where they live on the new tab

Favourites are Vault links you star (Vault ⋮ → "Add to favourites"), with no separate bookmarks list. They show as tiles on the new tab, one tap to open. Fake, neutral sites; Corn's real colours and sizes (16 dp corners, 52 dp tiles). Shown: A your idea (Vault fixed, favourites scroll), B a grid, C a card like Stats, then how starring works, what a new user sees, and Corny above the tagline.
